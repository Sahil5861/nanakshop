A LITTLE ABOUT US

Supporting the hands
that serve others.

Nanak Shop is here to help Gurudwaras and community kitchens source the raw materials that make langar possible. From aata and daal to rice and cooking essentials, we aim to make supply planning simpler.

Langar represents equality, generosity and community. We want ordering essentials to feel straightforward, dependable and centred on seva.
