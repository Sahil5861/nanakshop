Privacy Policy

How this demo handles information, and what must be configured before launch.

Last updated: 15 October 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy template explains the intended handling of information submitted through the Nanak Shop website. It must be updated to match the production website, actual data flows, service providers and applicable legal requirements.

2. Information you enter

The demo checkout form asks for your name, phone number, Gurudwara or organisation, delivery area/address, notes and selected products. These details are assembled into a JSON object in your browser.

3. Local storage

The demo stores the cart selection in your browser's localStorage so items remain in the cart after a page refresh. The cart is stored on the device/browser being used and is not automatically sent to Nanak Shop. You can clear it by removing the items or clearing this site's browser storage.

4. JSON enquiry download

When the demo checkout form is submitted, the website generates and downloads a JSON file containing the submitted form fields, selected items and submission metadata. The file is saved by your browser. The demo does not upload it to a server, email it, or submit it to an order-management system.

5. How information may be used in production

Once the website is connected to a backend, customer and order details may be used to respond to enquiries, confirm availability, arrange deliveries, manage orders and provide customer support. The final site should clearly identify who can access the information, the lawful basis for processing where applicable, and how long records are retained.

6. Sharing and service providers

The demo does not transmit checkout data to third parties. It loads frontend libraries, fonts and images from external providers, which may receive standard technical request data such as IP address and browser information. The production policy should list the actual hosting, analytics, messaging, payment and other providers used.

7. Security and retention

Browser localStorage is not an encrypted or secure vault. Avoid using the demo on shared devices for sensitive information. A production version should use suitable access controls, secure transport, validation, server-side protection, retention rules and backup practices.

8. Your choices and requests

You may clear cart data from your browser. When a production order system is implemented, provide a verified contact channel for privacy requests, corrections or questions and explain the process for handling them.

9. Children

This website is intended for general business and community use and is not designed to collect children's personal information knowingly.

10. Policy updates and contact

This policy may be updated as the website evolves. Add the client's verified business contact details before publishing the final website.

This is starter copy, not legal advice. Verify the actual data handling and have the final policy reviewed before launch.
