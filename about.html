OUR STORY

Rooted in the spirit
of langar.

Langar is the Sikh tradition of serving a free community meal to all, without distinction. It reflects equality, humility, generosity and the belief that no one should be turned away from a shared meal.

Nanak Shop's purpose is to support this tradition by helping Gurudwaras and community kitchens source everyday ingredients such as aata, daal, rice and other kitchen essentials. By bringing essential items together in one place, we aim to make routine supply planning easier for the people who organise and serve meals.

This demo page describes the intended purpose and values of Nanak Shop. The business's founding date, founder story and verified milestones should be added once confirmed by the client.
